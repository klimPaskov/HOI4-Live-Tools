# Nationalist China FAIR showcase

Objective: unify China including warlords and communists, expel and capitulate
Japan, recover lost territory and finish as a top-tier power. Normal difficulty,
vanilla rules, historical AI and FAIR profile apply. Do not substitute Communist
China or an unrelated tag because a familiar old guide uses it.

## Define the measurable end state first

Record the actual Nationalist player ID, all starting Chinese core/claim and
unification targets from ordinary game content, lost-territory goals, and the
required state IDs. Define which goals require direct ownership, control or
lawful integration; a friendly independent warlord is not unification. Taiwan,
Manchuria and other claims must be checked from the installed game, not a
remembered map. Do not invent new territorial scope from real-world politics.

Record exact power thresholds before starting. Unless the user supplied targets,
use these showcase defaults: independent; in-game major where status is
observable; at least 100 usable civilian plus military factories, including at
least 60 military; at least 100 deployed combat divisions averaging 90% equipment
strength with no theater-wide critical supply alert; at least 500 mission-ready
combat aircraft; and naval transport/escort capacity demonstrated by a supplied
home-island operation. Require nonnegative essential-equipment net trends for
30 game days after the final offensive. These are house targets, not definitions
of the engine's major status. Record baseline and final counts.

All declared unification states must be integrated/owned, Japan capitulated with
settlement proof, no declared target state occupied by an enemy, and no active
war against a remaining enemy major. Use the visible war ledger; do not estimate
hidden enemy capability. Unknown metrics remain unmet evidence. If installed
content warrants different numeric targets, declare them before day one with
the reason; never lower them late to make the run successful.

## Build/DLC branch

The [NCNS release notes](sources.md#china-and-modern-systems) explicitly changed
Nationalist China, warlord composition and doctrine systems. Enumerate the
actual tags, focus branches, political mechanics and national debuffs. With
older content use its displayed reform/unification route; with NCNS use the
installed KMT political, reform and assistance systems. Never hardcode an old
Subjugate the Warlords queue into a tree that does not contain it.

Prioritize removal of army/economic penalties that materially block survival,
rifle output, essential support and early supply. Meet reform XP requirements
through intended exercises/combat without wasting the equipment needed to hold.
Do not fund a showcase navy or heavy armor before the defense can survive.

## Survive the invasion

Use affordable equipped infantry, selected support/AA, short defensible lines
and reserve divisions behind vital hubs. Guard valuable ports with dedicated
units and a mobile local response. A coast-defense plan must still cover ports
after main armies move. Do not spread the army into every beach while leaving
the northern front empty. Avoid unlimited recruitment that starves existing
divisions of rifles and trains.

Before Japan attacks, pursue viable unification/coalition choices without
creating an unwinnable simultaneous internal war. If necessary prioritize the
external invasion and postpone final internal settlement; record the pending
objective. Use normal foreign assistance when useful, accounting for actual
delivery and trade/convoy risk. The FAIR profile does not ban genuine help.

Hold defensible terrain, rotate exhausted units, repair supply and attack only
where a pocket or port can be taken. Counter Japanese landings before a supplied
bridgehead expands; isolate them from their port when feasible. Do not leave a
port empty repeatedly to farm artificial invasions. Accept a controlled retreat
to preserve the field army if the northern line becomes untenable.

## Reverse the war and finish Japan

Once equipment/reforms/supply permit, concentrate an offensive group on a short
axis, close pockets and advance through useful hubs. Do not push the entire
army into low-supply terrain at once. Recover lost northern territory in bounds,
then verify mainland Japanese holdings and remaining client enemies.

Expelling Japan from the mainland is an intermediate milestone, not Japanese
capitulation. Reject an optional peace that ends the current route to capitulation
unless a feasible lawful later route is explicitly recorded before accepting.
Peace itself cannot discharge the capitulation objective. If an unavoidable
settlement occurs, record it and use lawful future options; never force an event
to keep the war open.

Develop the transports, convoys, air, escorts and staging ports for the home
islands as the land war becomes secure. Use the shared invasion state machine;
verify range and every sea region, secure a usable port, protect supply and
reinforce only within throughput. Do not claim that a fleet assignment grants
supremacy or that aircraft numbers alone guarantee a launch. Accept assistance
and basing only through ordinary game diplomacy and player-visible information.

## Complete unification and prove power

Resolve warlords and communist opposition through eligible focus, diplomacy,
integration or a planned conventional war. Recheck states at peace conferences;
an ally may hold a required state. Maintain occupation/garrison supply and
rebuild damaged industry. End with a sustainable army and positive critical
production, not a hollow manpower total.

Show territorial checklist, Japanese capitulation and settlement, independence,
major/rank evidence if available, remaining wars, industrial growth and force
readiness. Distinguish achieved, missing and unknown metrics explicitly.
