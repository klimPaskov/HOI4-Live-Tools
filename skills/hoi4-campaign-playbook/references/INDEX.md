## Ten rules that decided past failed campaigns
1. Equipment, not division count, wins: size training to equipment production.
2. Draw fronts for EVERY border and coast before declaring any war.
3. Spend political power; never bank it past a purchase you need.
4. Keep research slots, focus, construction and production lines full.
5. Watch consumer goods and supply; fix before they hurt.
6. Start the main war early (1939), not 1941.
7. Verify unit positions, not just orders.
8. Answer event pop-ups promptly; log the event title.
9. Keep home defence; never send every army abroad.
10. Verify every action with a read; never retry uncertain input blindly.
