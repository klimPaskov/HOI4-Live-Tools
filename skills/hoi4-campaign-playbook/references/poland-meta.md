# Poland META showcase

This is the third campaign, explicitly META. Normal/vanilla/historical AI and
all common campaign integrity restrictions remain. The player may choose an
alternate-history Polish route. META permits normal-UI exploits and strong
metas; it does not require a bug when intended mechanics win more reliably.

If the user has not supplied a more specific Polish endpoint, record before
day one: survive independently, capitulate Germany and the USSR and obtain a
settlement leaving no active war with either, restore the declared starting
Polish core-state checklist and secure a connected Polish-led settlement.
Demonstrate at least one consequential META-permitted tactic with before/after
evidence. Before using it, name a measurable operation it should help achieve
(a specified hub/port/VP captured, pocket closed or defended front held through
a specified enemy offensive); record the actual contribution, cost and outcome.
A token template edit is not a demonstration. End at a conclusive peace;
world conquest is optional only if separately assigned. Preserve any user-set
goals instead of replacing them with this default.

## Choose a route from the installed tree

Identify DLC, focuses, visible event choices and deadlines. Compare paths by
industry/cores gained before the existential war, diplomatic control of the
two-front problem and time required to recover from political change. The
[2021 developer diaries](sources.md#poland) establish several alternate routes;
they are not proof of today's strongest route or of a current exploit.

Useful candidates to inspect are a Commonwealth-oriented expansion/integration
route, a Habsburg route where Czech/Hungarian gains are actually available, or
a Romanov route with a large eastern ceiling if its real event chain is known.
Prefer the strongest verified route that can be executed promptly; do not spend
years cycling unknown monarch events or invent a hidden option. Resolve focus
IDs/prerequisites and journal the selected branch before locking it in.

A pragmatic baseline is to expand core industry/territory through the available
monarchist/unification content while managing Germany diplomatically, defeat
one major opponent, then turn the enlarged economy against the other. Giving
up a demanded state temporarily is permitted when it prevents a fatal two-front
war and the objective includes recovering it later; inspect the actual demand,
guarantees and future consequences. Never assume ceding Danzig guarantees peace
forever or that historical AI will ignore its remaining goals.

## Build for asymmetric survival

Secure rifles, support/AA, useful artillery, trains/trucks and a reserve before
luxury expansion. Choose research and doctrine to make the affordable force
effective. Avoid a civilian-only opening that produces no usable army by war.
Concentrate on terrain and supply where the enemy cannot use all its strength;
retreat from exposed salients before they become traps. Use defensive fighting
to exhaust attacks, then counterattack toward hubs and encirclements.

META can concentrate armor in selected infantry divisions if this yields useful
visible armor/piercing behavior at acceptable cost. Duplicate a template, add
the available line armor type, check equipment demand and resulting stats, then
convert only a small equipped subset first. Observe actual battle losses and
production burden before scaling. This is the space-marine house-rule exception,
not an assertion that it is currently overpowered or a guaranteed exploit.

Use powerful designer/MIO/leader/doctrine combinations where the installed
rules grant them, choosing outcomes by cost and performance. Do not declare a
combo best because an old guide says so. Keep ordinary troops filling the front
while the expensive group does the work that justifies its equipment.

## META opportunity cards

| Candidate | Operational use | What to verify before depending on it |
|---|---|---|
| Early focus war / core snowball | Add a tractable neighbor's economy before major war | Actual war goal, guarantees, faction response, cores and force/time cost |
| Hybrid armor infantry | Hold/punch above the industrial budget in selected sectors | Displayed armor/piercing, supply/fuel, armor stock and battle outcome |
| Airborne port or VP rush | Bypass an expensive front and accelerate capitulation | Airlift capacity, route conditions, target intel from normal play, unit survival and actual surrender progress |
| Subject templates and manpower | Sustain expansion with normal subject systems | Autonomy rules, current ownership of equipment/manpower, recruitment delivery and long-term cost |
| AI bait and concentrated counterattack | Draw a local overextension and close a pocket | Own escape route, supply, reserves and enemy movement actually visible |
| UI conversion/cap/mastery exploit | Only if a reproducible working build-specific method is found | Exact normal-UI recipe, source/version, bounded cost, visible postcondition; otherwise leave candidate unused |

For airborne operations, check current air-superiority/transport requirements
and range in the launch tooltip. Do not hardcode an old transport count. A
capital landing can fail to capitulate a country and can destroy the entire
force. Build a fallback; do not reload to make it succeed.

Do not silently promote internet allegations into a working exploit. The
official [1.10.6 notes](sources.md#poland) already removed encircled template
swapping in 2021, illustrating why old recipes need verification. Never waste a
live campaign trying a patched behavior repeatedly.

## Defeat the majors and settle

Destroy field formations before chasing distant capitals. Take connected hubs,
airfields and industrial areas, reorganizing after each annexation/peace. Keep
a reserve against the second major. Once the first is defeated, integrate gains,
repair logistics and move the offensive arm with enough time to recover.

For the USSR, extend supply in bounds and pocket armies; avoid a long unsupported
drive east. For Germany, protect against breakthroughs while concentrating
assault power on accessible industrial/VP axes. If the Axis or Allies remain
at war, list the actual major enemies and plan the required later theaters.

Record normal-UI META techniques actually used, failed candidates and measured
effects. End evidence includes independence, both major defeats, required
territory/peace, unresolved wars, economy and force condition. A flashy tactic
or political formable is not a substitute for the recorded victory predicates.
