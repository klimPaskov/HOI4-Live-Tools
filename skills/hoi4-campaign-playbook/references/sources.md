# Sources and version checks

Research checked 11 October 2026. These sources support specific context below;
they do not validate a live run. Strategy and review thresholds elsewhere are
this playbook's decision guidance, not quoted community rules or guaranteed
optimal builds. No source code or complete guide was copied.

## Multiplayer rules

- [Paradox Frontlines Basic Ruleset](https://assets.ctfassets.net/u73tyf0fa8v1/6QZCio5m4P4FU4iKEiy0aS/33cfa33b01f54f5eeb122d3f64e01901/Hearts_of_Iron_IV__Frontlines_-_Basic_Ruleset.pdf),
  undated document, accessed 11 October 2026; official Frontlines foundational
  guidance. It forbids unfair exploits including factory boosting, artificial
  tension and excessive puppet releases, discourages needless pauses and defers
  to house rules. Its human-player dogpiling rule is not imposed on AI showcases.
  [Official Frontlines hub](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/hearts-of-iron-iv/tournaments).
- [hoi4 mp gaming historical rules](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/hoi4-mp-gaming/discussions/0/360671727323376942/),
  9 August 2016 with revisions/comments that month. Primary published community
  house rules; historical evidence for war windows, exploit bans and objections
  to factory-transfer trading or disbanding threatened units. Numeric limits and
  balance claims are obsolete unless independently revalidated. The July 1939
  opening used here is an explicit house choice, not a universal rule.
- [Kaiserreich Multiplayer rules](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/KMPGMP/discussions/0/1333474229065785543/),
  11 May 2017, edited 8 September 2017. Primary community rules specifically for
  a mod, including a space-marine ban. This is evidence that such bans exist,
  not evidence of a current vanilla exploit or a vanilla community consensus.

No up-to-date universal multiplayer consensus was established. The FAIR table
is an explicit conservative showcase profile, while META is the user's distinct
single-player profile. Do not convert host-specific rules into engine laws.

## Before using a patch-dependent recommendation

1. Match country, build, DLC and relevant mechanic to the source. If they differ,
   treat the source as a hypothesis, never as an action-ready instruction.
2. Inspect available ordinary UI catalogs/tooltips or installed static game
   definitions for public rules, prerequisites, IDs and costs. Static rules
   inspection may clarify mechanics; it must not expose hidden live enemy state
   or enable script execution in the campaign.
3. Evaluate the cost, earliest payoff, risk to current objectives and mode rules.
   A powerful tactic may be FAIR; a UI bug remains META even if not very strong.
4. For META bugs, record a bounded normal-UI recipe and expected readback before
   trying it. Keep unverified candidates out of the critical path.
5. Update the run journal with actual effects and failures. Do not rewrite the
   source date or claim this skill has validated current gameplay by existing.

For source maintenance, prefer official released notes and original community
rulebooks. A developer preview is weaker evidence for exact current mechanics;
a creator's old success is weaker evidence for a current exploit. Search results
and third-party AI summaries are discovery aids, not a verified recipe.
