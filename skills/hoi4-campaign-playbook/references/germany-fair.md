# Germany FAIR showcase

Objective from the Mac handoff: Anschluss and Czechoslovakia, defeat Poland,
Low Countries and France, successful Sea Lion and UK capitulation, form Greater
German Reich, capitulate USSR and defeat all Allies. Record the final peace.
Use the FAIR profile and Normal/vanilla/historical AI conditions.

## Before day one

Resolve the installed German focus tree, national spirits and formable decision
requirements. Record needed state IDs and whether each needs ownership or
control. Different DLC/builds change these conditions. Read the current German
economy tooltips: [2024 developer material](sources.md#germany) describes MEFO
and conquest-economy changes; its development numbers are not current constants.

Allocate an economical western holding army, the main eastern field army, a
mobile assault group, port guards and a reserve. Create air and naval theater
plans. Keep infantry equipment flowing while expanding artillery/support,
fighters, CAS, armor, trucks/trains and convoys in achievable proportions.

## 1936–1938: prepare without empty growth

Prioritize industrial research and bonuses that pay before war, then military
equipment needed by the force. Build early civilian capacity only while it
supports a timely military transition; calculate from the actual economy and
war deadline rather than blindly building civilians until a remembered date.
Keep focus prerequisites and required fielded strength on schedule using real
equipped divisions, not empty-template manpower inflation.

Pursue Anschluss and the Czech diplomatic chain when available. The normal
successful path is preferable to an unplanned Austrian war. If a demand fails,
read the offered consequence, journal it, and plan a real war if required;
never fire the event or reload for a better roll. Avoid stacking the whole army
on one congested capital approach. Retain western coverage at every step.

Watch consumer-goods changes, PP obligations, available construction capacity
and resources as national spirits evolve. Use the economy path's intended
decisions; do not assume conquest automatically removes the economic burden.
Keep reserve trade options for rubber/oil and a plan for wartime sea disruption.

## Poland and the western campaign

The FAIR house window begins July 1939; choose readiness within/after it.
Verify western defense before declaring. Aim to destroy Polish formations via
short converging thrusts and secure hubs/VP routes. Do not attack every tile
with understrength infantry. After victory, verify surrender/war state and
reassign the eastern reserve before moving the assault group west.

Prepare airfields and supply for the Low Countries route. Keep the French
border pinned/defended while concentrated mobile forces create breakthroughs
through viable northern terrain. Follow with infantry, close pockets and keep
the spearhead supplied. Check the changing frontline after each capitulation;
orders tied to the old border may become wasteful. Resolve France's actual
capitulation/occupation outcome and preserve access needed for the next phase.

## Sea Lion

Use the invasion state machine in [strategy](strategy.md). Select a route from
owned, supplied ports to a supportable British port and nearby landing tiles.
Have transport capability, escorts, fuel, invasion troops, a follow-on army and
air cover before launch. Use naval/air pressure to create a real crossing window;
do not depend on the AI momentarily forgetting to cover a sea zone.

Wait for observed launch conditions on every route region. After landing,
capture and defend a port, repair it, escort reinforcement, and move aircraft
into range. Expand toward meaningful victory points and supply while retaining
the beachhead guard. If the landing fails, accept the loss, preserve remaining
forces and rebuild the operation; no reload. UK capitulation must appear in
fresh evidence. It does not prove the Allies as a whole are defeated.

## USSR and Allied completion

Avoid launching an eastern offensive while the previous theater is consuming
all reserves. Build/repair supply axes, motorize necessary hubs, deploy planes
within range and ensure fuel/equipment buffers. Advance by hub and pocket,
leaving infantry to hold the widening front. Do not chase distant victory points
with isolated tanks or grind through winter/mud without local advantage.

Recheck Greater German Reich requirements as territories change; finish the
ordinary decision only when eligible. Do not infer completion from map color.
After Soviet capitulation list every remaining enemy major and faction war.
Plan additional naval/logistical theaters as necessary to finish the Allies.
Do not end the showcase at London, Moscow, a celebratory event, or five hours
if the recorded objectives remain unmet.

## Evidence checklist

Record each diplomatic acquisition, Polish/French/UK/Soviet capitulation,
British landing and supplied bridgehead, the formable decision/result,
remaining-war list and final peace ownership. Capture failures and loss of
objectives too. Success means all recorded predicates are true, not merely that
the player's army is large or the front is green.
