# Nation openers

What strong players do in the first years with each nation, as a starting point for a plan. Dates assume the 1936
start; a focus takes 70 days (cost 10) or 35 (cost 5): Germany, Italy, the USSR, Japan and Manchukuo have many
35-day focuses, the UK and USA few. "Hold" a focus means keeping a short gap (up to about 10 days) to buy something
first. PP buys are in brackets after the focus they follow. Read the tag's current tree, laws and
decisions before copying a step: DLCs change trees. FAIR showcases keep historical war timings (Germany: no general
European war before July 1939); steps marked META are allowed only in a META run. Tactic ids (PR08, PO19, ...)
are in [tactics.md](tactics.md).

Every opener starts the same way on day 0, still paused: put free military factories on existing lines (PR01),
cull start lines the plan does not use and settle the start naval queue (PR07, PR08), reset inherited trades to
what the lines need (EC16), queue construction in the best states, pick the first focus and research, and hire
the chief of army among the first three buys (PO07). Strong multiplayer openers add: train the start air wings and
most of the start fleet for XP (stop 30 days before war, AR16), promote every general to field marshal, create the
agency on day 1, put the first scientist on a land project, and set the occupation law on held states.

Mods: some mods cut infantry and special-forces width to about 1.8 per battalion, add passive-defence levels or
change fort and faction-goal counts. Keep the battalion mix and re-fit the width to the installed game (AR01, AR23).

Start laws: Germany, Italy, Japan, Poland and Hungary start on Partial Mobilisation; the USSR, UK, France and
Romania on Civilian Economy; the USA on Undisturbed Isolation with the Great Depression and Free Trade. Research
slots at start: Germany, Italy, UK and USA 4; USSR, Japan, France, Poland, Hungary and Romania 3; China 2.
Balance of power is active only for DEN, ETH, FIN, ITA, PRC, SWE and SWI; BRA and CZE have one only with the Trials of Allegiance and Peace for Our Time trees, which this install lacks. Trees in this install (10 DLCs plus
the integrated Together for Victory, Death or Dishonor, Waking the Tiger and Man the Guns): AFG, ARG, BRA, BUL, CHL,
GRE, INS, IRQ, PAR, PER, SIA, TUR and URG play the generic tree (`generic_focus`), so never name a country focus for
them; HUN plays the Götterdämmerung tree (`wuw_HUN_*`), MAN the NCNS tree (`MAN_tsr_*`); ROM, YUG, CZE, HOL, MEX and
the dominions keep their own trees. Check the tag's tree before using an opener.

## Germany (GER)

Full FAIR plan with checkpoints: [germany-fair.md](germany-fair.md).
- **Start:** Limited Conscription, Limited Exports, Partial Mobilisation; MEFO bills (consumer goods +10%, rising about
  3% a month, price controls slow it).
- **Day 0:** every military factory on infantry equipment (enough rifles for Anschluss with real divisions); remove the
  light-tank and old fighter lines; keep the Type II subs, Z1934s and Deutschland, remove Hipper and both Scharnhorst
  unless the plan wants capitals; train most of the fleet (the capital ships patrol the home coast for dominance) and
  the air wings; promote the generals to field marshals; agency; the land-facility scientist on the engineering
  vehicle project; civilian factories in Hessen, Westfalen and Moselland; research machine tools, construction,
  electronics.
- **Focus and PP 1936-37:** Rhineland (Political Loyalty, Free Trade), Führerprinzip, Reichsleiter Todt, Reorganize the
  Wehrmacht (War Economy, Schacht, the Reich Marshal), Anschluss (Krupp) about 30 days after Reorganize with Free Trade
  and core coastal protection, Sudetenland after the Sino-Japanese war starts, Hess, First Vienna Award (army drill
  expert), Fate of Czechoslovakia, Four Year Plan (pick Speer in its Captain of Industry event), Reichsautobahn,
  Goebbels (replaces Todt), KdF-Wagen, Heraeus (Seldte), Accelerate Rearmament, Concentrated Armament, Production
  Targets, Autarky (Limited Exports), Institute Price Controls (every price-control decision except Gömbös).
- **Focus and PP 1938-39:** Reassert Eastern Claims, Modern Maneuver Warfare, New Panzer Doctrine, the medium-tank
  research focus, Molotov-Ribbentrop (so the USSR does not take Memel), Coal Liquefaction (Develop Ersatz Material),
  Danzig or War no earlier than July 1939 (Military High Command, logistics advisor, Total Mobilisation), Around
  Maginot (Funk as soon as he is available), Buna-Werke, Zentrale Planung, Armament Rationalization, Loyalty to the
  Führer (War Bonds, renewed on expiry), Rüstungsstab after the gold decisions bring consumer goods near 10%, Swiss
  Gold; then Panzergrenadier, Nationalize Ford, Bribe Senior Officers, Kriegsschulen, Innovative Warfare, Salvage
  Captured Equipment, the Luftwaffe research focuses, Panzer Troops School, Advanced Panzer Research, Dive Bombers,
  Kummersdorf, Combined Arms, Improve the Logistics System. Extensive Conscription late 1938.
- **Research (RE19-RE21):** the two 100% industry bonuses (Four Year Plan, Autarky Efforts) on Tools 3 and Tools 4;
  Concentrated 3-4 and Excavation 2-3 on the faction's 50% bonuses (Concentrated 4 mid-1938); the medium-focus 100% on
  the improved medium chassis mid-1938; no line-artillery equipment techs (radar instead); AT ahead of time from
  mid-1937 so AT 3 lands about January 1941; Radio 3 March 1940; mechanised early; then gun, AA and radar passives and
  Mechanised 3.
- **Construction:** civilian factories until Todt leaves the cabinet, then military factories until November 1938
  (Develop Western and Central Germany decisions in June 1937); about 12 refineries from July 1938 (industrial MIO on
  the chemical designer while building them, back to the construction MIO after; cancel rubber imports by mid-1938),
  then military factories again; an air facility and radar at war. Where bombing is allowed: state AA in the Ruhr and
  Rhineland and max radar there. Build military factories in captured Poland and western France before clicking their
  gold decisions (EC24).
- **Production:** rifles to Anschluss, then the rifle line down; mechanised 13 (August 1937), infantry equipment II 10
  (February 1938). At about 175 military factories (May 1939): medium TD 70, medium tanks 50, mechanised 20, AT 10,
  support 5, armoured support 3, flame 2, recon plane 1. Early 1940: CAS to 60, flame, light recon and engineering
  vehicles 10 each, AA trucks and trains; then tanks to 100-150 with mechanised 40, TD 60, heavy tanks 40-45 for a
  coastal defence or Barbarossa build, super-heavy howitzers 3-5, AA 10.
- **MIOs (RE22):** tank designer policy Assembly Line Optimisers from mid-1938, Armor Welding Specialists from late
  1939 once its tanks stop; Cutting Corners on the main tank designer; move tank lines to the second tank designer once
  the first reaches level 10; own mechanised first as a cost-only variant without the MIO, MIO after (PR38).
- **Agency and faction:** agency day 1: Suicide Pills, Invisible Ink, Form Department, Radio Interception x2 (decrypt
  France and the UK), Passive Defence x4; advisors who add a spy slot go to counter-intelligence and cryptology; Army
  Department and Interrogation in 1938; cryptology techs, networks on the USSR, UK and USA in 1940. Collaboration
  governments in order: France 3, Poland 3, Belgium 2, Netherlands 2, Denmark 2, Norway 2 (none in the USSR: FAIR Axis
  rule). Faction goals DI19.
- **Army:** about 30 divisions in 1936, 60 by end 1937, 95+ by mid-1939; separate armour and infantry divisions;
  38-wide armour (templates T16); volunteers to Spain for traits. Doctrine: bank manpower with Irregulars and Desperate
  Defence first, then Grand Battleplan with Individual Excellence, Siege Artillery, Mobile Defense and Grand Assault;
  no Women in the Workforce. Field marshals: Combined Arms Expert, Fortress Buster, Offensive Doctrine, Ranger,
  Camouflage Expert, Improvisation Expert; the planner (Guderian type): Organizer, Logistics Wizard, Thorough Planner,
  Aggressive Assaulter.
- **Navy and air:** 10-20 dockyards on torpedo destroyers from 1936 (30-40 by mid-1939), 1-2 convoy lines; from 1939
  improved-hull U-boats (60-100) on 8-12-boat groups in deep ocean; 300-600 naval bombers by mid-1940 for the Channel;
  Kriegsmarine chain `GER_strengthen_the_kriegsmarine`, `GER_trade_interdiction`, `GER_wolfpack_tactics` (needs war),
  `GER_u_boat_efforts`. Fighters from about 5 factories early to 15-25 by 1939; a large CAS force from 1940. Fighter:
  three heavy MG, engine III, Focke-Wulf (range-focused MIO); one armour plate keeps about 1,200 km for Africa, two
  plates elsewhere.
- **War timing:** Danzig or War no earlier than July 1939 in FAIR; Poland without calling allies; then justify on France
  so Belgium joins with it, leaving only the Netherlands and Luxembourg to justify (skips Around Maginot); Norway and
  Denmark by justification rather than the focus; seize gold after each capitulation. Barbarossa supply from February

- **Start:** Disarmed Nation, Free Trade; Undisturbed Isolation (50% consumer goods, -50% factory and dockyard
  construction, isolation raises the justify and guarantee gates); Great Depression (+1 PP a day cost, -50% recruitable).
- **PP order:** Financial Expert (Morgenthau), offense chief, Silent Workhorse (Taft), Amend the Budget, a scientist,
  Improve Worker Conditions once, Partial Mobilisation, War Industrialist (Stimson), more Amend the Budget, the vehicle
  designer after Wartime Industry, the Economist (Perlo, after the Ware Group focus), the Armaments Organiser (Nelson)
  for Total Mobilisation, Women in the Workforce at war; before the war the high command: infantry expert, commando
  expert, tactical-bombing air advisor and the air chief.
- **Focus:** Continue the New Deal, WPA, Agricultural Adjustment Act; if Germany took Anschluss early: Neutrality Act,
  Arsenal of Democracy, The Giant Wakes (Guerilla War); otherwise Scientific R&D Office first. Then Fair Labor
  Standards (230-day Depression gate), War Department, Air War Plans Division, Air Support, Federal Housing Act (ends
  the Depression), Escort Fighters (+300% heavy fighter), Scientist Haven (research slot), Wartime Industry, Military
  Construction (slots and military factories in a big state), Selective Training Act, Ware Group, Louisiana
  Maneuvers, Airborne Divisions; later War Plan Black, Rock Island (AT 3 bonus), Department of Defense (command power
  for landings).
- **Research:** heavy fighter on day 1 and the late heavy fighter with the Escort Fighters bonus; strategic bombers
  1936-37; computing machine; concentrated industry, one slot on industry and one on tools all game; Construction 3;
  excavation and aluminium; own AT 3 about February 1941; the faction's two 50% industry bonuses on Tools 4 and
  Concentrated 4; for landings: Radio 3, Armour 3, light and medium chassis for support tanks, flame, own Gun 3 and the
  last gun passive, Marines 2, Rangers 2 or Mountaineers 2, own mechanised, AT passives.
- **Construction:** infrastructure and facilities first (isolation slows only factories and dockyards); civilian
  factories toward 150 owned (the Industrial Expansion faction goal), then military factories from about January 1938
  for a strategic-bomber line and fighters; facility in a state with one more infrastructure level.
- **Production:** strategic bombers 20 early (aluminium-free, AI29); every other early military factory on fighters
  (heavy fighters: advanced airframe rushed with the bonuses, four or more heavy MG, the jet engine as soon as possible); trucks and trains 2;
  about 25 mechanised for the marines; support tanks (light recon 15-20% above the medium flame line); never 300
  factories on rifles with a 200k stockpile: build tanks, bombers or amphibious gear.
- **1939-41:** join the Allies only on a FAIR trigger (Japanese attack, 1942, an invasion of the UK or the Americas,
  or two of the three Soviet cities lost); late civilian-to-military conversion is nearly free
  (-50% focus, armaments organiser, Total Mobilisation): about 60 civilian factories converted in mid-1941. Decrypt
  and infiltrate Bulgaria, Italy, Germany. Hawaii: 10 good divisions, air and CAS over it, subs raiding around it.
- **Landing in France:** NA38-NA41 and templates T18-T19; land on a big port with a peninsula behind it (Brest:
  reserve waves land behind to encircle, best for Allied air); after landfall push from several directions (Low
  Countries, southern France, Norway); rangers on fronts, not on battle plans across rivers (OP49).
- **META:** an early war to drop the Depression; civ boosting to allies (MX08).
- **Gates:** the Two Ocean Navy Act adds 5 dockyards each in two states with 5 free slots: keep those slots free.
  Limited Intervention, War Propaganda and Focus on Europe open the intervention-mandate decisions; the war goal on
  Germany needs war support above 25% and congress support.

## France (FRA)

- **Start:** Limited Conscription, Export Focus, Civilian Economy; 8 military, 33 civilian factories; Disjointed
  Government (+0.8 PP daily cost, -10% stability, -50% surrender limit); Full Employment (going past Limited
  Conscription turns it into a -10% output Worker Shortage).
- **1936:** Local Autonomy; agency; civilian factories in Normandy, Loire, Champagne (infrastructure in Calais first);
  Revive the National Bloc, Laissez-Faire (three 150% industry bonuses on completion: hold them for tools and
  concentrated industry; two years later Inefficient Economy steps down from -20% to -10% output); take the 1 May 1936
  election event (free Early Mobilisation); Review Foreign Policy, Silent Workhorse; dockyards on convoys.
- **1937-38:** Begin Rearmament; the flame-tank project (cheap design now, converted later); attaché to China;
  Strengthen Government (PP-starved until 1938); Blum-Viollette, Encourage Immigration (removes Full Employment before
  Extensive Conscription); Defensive Stratagems a year after Strengthen Government (removes Disjointed Government);
  bank 300 PP for War Economy and Extensive Conscription on war day.
- **1939:** join the Allies; Colonial Industry (+12 factories); against the AI, counter-attack into the Rhineland with
  tanks or SP guns while Germany is in Poland.
- **Third Empire:** Repeal the Law of Exile (+120 PP) also removes Disjointed Government once the 365-day
  constitutional revision has expired; join the Allies before proclaiming the Third Empire.

## Hungary (HUN)

  treaty (-15% PP).
- **1936-37:** railroad reintegration, Imrédy or Ganz, Secret Rearmament, then Partial Mobilisation at once; Horthy
  consolidation; bank rearmament manpower and train by exercises; renounce Trianon; Győr Programme (academy, mines,

  France falls; Yugoslavia with Germany in 1941; joining the Allies cuts rubber, so build air first or refineries.

  Burgenland 185k before the Anschluss, Transylvania 200k); a refusal gives a war goal; meet the gates with real
  infantry (one-battalion spam is META, MX21).
